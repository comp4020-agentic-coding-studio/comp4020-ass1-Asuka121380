# Where the Beat Leans

Eight notes can be perfectly even and still feel weightless. Shift the accent,
introduce a backbeat, let the kick and bass lock together or answer one another,
and the same small measure begins to lean, pull and move.

*Where the Beat Leans* is an interactive exhibition about that change. Across
five guided acts, a bare pulse gradually becomes a conversation between a drum
kit and a bass. Handwritten notes invite you to listen, alter the score and hear
each musical decision take effect; the final laboratory then leaves the pattern
in your hands. No theory lesson comes first. The exhibition begins with the ear
and lets the notation explain what you have already felt.
